## 👥 2. 5 User Personas & Blind Spot Synthesis

1. 🎯 **The Minimalist Productivity User**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Khôngรบกวน, Zero distraction, Click-through / ghost mode, Zen/Calm animation mode.
2. 🐱 **The Desk Pet & Cozy Gamer**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Emotional feedback, petting reactions, AFK snooze, gentle streak milestones.
3. 💻 **The Laptop & Battery Saver User**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Battery saver mode (12 FPS throttle on DC power), crisp pixel scaling on High-DPI screens.
4. 🎮 **The Power User & Multi-Monitor Gamer**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Multi-monitor coordinate memory, auto-hide on Fullscreen 3D games, global hotkeys.
5. 🛠️ **The Modder & Pixel Artist**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Drop-in folder for custom skins (`skins/`), JSON frame manifest, transparent PNGs.
6. 🌐 **The Web Researcher & Browser Multi-Tabber**:
   - *Key Requirement*: Side Panel Companion (Chrome/Edge/Firefox), Tab-Overload visual feedback (stack of carrots/books when 20+ tabs are open), and New Tab Lo-fi dashboard.
